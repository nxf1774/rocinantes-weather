Affordance notes

  • Forecast tabs are Daily, then Hourly. Daily is the open tab. Current is not in this mock.
  • The pills and the day/night write-up share one tray. Closed, that card is the pills plus the lip. Open, the same card lengthens and the write-up sits in it. There is no second panel.
  • The lip is a chevron only. Closed, it points down and opens Today’s forecast. Open, it points up and closes. There is no grabber, and no “Tap a day” or “Slide up” label.
  • Tapping a day slides that day’s write-up down inside the card. The evening block shows the low on the right, the same size and dial color as the afternoon high. Tap the selected day again, the chevron, or Escape to close.
  • “Icon beside temp” (or ?icons=beside) puts the afternoon icon next to the high and the evening icon next to the low. “Temps only” is the default.
  • Another day, while open, swaps the write-up in place. It does not collapse and reopen. The handle is a tap control. Drag-to-dismiss is not in this mock.
  • The live forecast stage keeps a fixed height and scrolls the write-up inside the box. This study lets the tray use its natural height so the slide is visible. Hero gauges are omitted; the header, flood banner, and conditions card are here for scale.
